The counter workflow, not a rate app

Free gold-rate calculators stop at a number. Batta resolves old-gold value directly into the balance due on a specific new purchase, and prints a customer-facing derivation slip those apps don't offer.

Exchange = weight × purity × rate × (1 − wastage). Balance = new price − exchange.
